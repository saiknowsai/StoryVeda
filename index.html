The Four Vedas

Introducing children to Vedam

The Vedas are the world's oldest living wisdom. StoryVeda gently introduces each of the four Vedas through age-appropriate stories, songs and activities.

ऋग्वेद

Rigveda

The Veda of hymns and praise. Stories of creation, nature and the greatness of the divine — told through song and verse.

सामवेद

Samaveda

The Veda of music and melody. Children discover the power of sound, rhythm and sacred chanting through gentle songs.

यजुर्वेद

Yajurveda

The Veda of ritual and action. Stories of offering, gratitude and doing good — connecting children to meaningful practice.

अथर्ववेद

Atharvaveda

The Veda of healing and harmony. Stories of protection, courage and the quiet strength that comes from within.

ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। May we be protected together. May we be nourished together. — Taittiriya Upanishad
